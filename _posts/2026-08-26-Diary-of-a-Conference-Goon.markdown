For anyone that knows me, knows I like to volunteer for stuff; I help run a local [hackspace][1] as well as crewing at quite a few different community conferences such as [Bsides london][2], [Bsides leeds][3] and [steelcon][4]. I love crewing these conferences, and without volunteers stuff like this is very hard to pull off so it's something I like to do as a bit of a "be the change you want to see in the world".

## DEF CON
My first DEF CON was _technically_ 2020 (safe mode) and I think that's still the one I've seen the most talks from but the first one I came to in person was 32 (2024) [work][5] were kind enough to take a good chunk of the team out, and I was kind of nervous - I'd never been to the US before, truth be told I'd never flown for more than maybe 3 hours, so it was kind of daunting but after seeing some of the talks and talking to folks who'd been before it sounded pretty awesome - so off we went.

## The Vegas Heat
Having never been in a desert before I _really_ wasn't prepared for the heat... people had told me it's hot there, and it's not that I didn't believe them; like, intelletually I _know_ deserts are hot, but I wasn't really prepared to step out of the airport at 20:00 and be greeted with 35C/~90F heat; thankfully Ubers/Lyfts/Car Shares have air con :-) so, after a short ride to the hotel we got checked in. The hotel was also air conditioned but had a strange smell, I want to say guava or something similar but it was cool (no pun intended). The hotel was also basically on the monorail line so it was super easy to get to and from the LVCC and back (again with air conditioning)

## Surviving said Heat
Obviously when you're walking around a desert it's very important to stay hydrated and _covered_ in sunscreen - the higher the better for me at least, in my experience of vegas you're never too far from somewhere you can buy water/sunscreen/other drinks - water can be pretty expensive though if you're just grabbing a bottle from CVS or the like; what I took to doing was grabbing a couple of cans of Arizona Ice Tea (honey and ginseng) as it's $0.99 for a gigantic can and throwing them in my bag along with my double walled reusable water bottle - there are water refil stations through out the conference centre which is great!

A lot of folks recommend the 3-2-1 rule whilst at DEF CON that is every day you need (at least) 3 hours of sleep, 2 meals, and 1 shower - although you're milage may vary on the sleep and showers needless to say the more sleep the better you'll feel and I suppose the same rings true for showering.
